Ray–triangle intersection

A click is a window coordinate. Picking is a ray. The triangle answers with barycentric coordinates, or it answers no.

Implemented while studying lines, rays, and planes in Eric Lengyel, Foundations of Game Engine Development, Volume 1: Mathematics (Ch. 3), and vector geometry in Fletcher Dunn & Ian Parberry, 3D Math Primer for Graphics and Game Development. Intersection and visualization are my own — not book code or figures.
