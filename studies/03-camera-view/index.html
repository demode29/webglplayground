Camera / view matrix

The camera is not a picture. It is a change of basis: world points written in a frame that sits at the eye, looks at the target, and treats “up” as a vector you chose. Projection is the lens. View is the pose.

Implemented while studying Chapter 2 (Transforms, coordinate spaces) of Eric Lengyel, Foundations of Game Engine Development, Volume 1: Mathematics, and the camera / matrix chapters of Fletcher Dunn & Ian Parberry, 3D Math Primer for Graphics and Game Development. The orbiting scene is my own.
