Quaternion rotation

Euler angles are three knobs. At ±90° pitch two of them turn the same axis. A quaternion is one rotation: an axis and an angle, with no privileged sequence.

Implemented while studying Chapter 2.7 (Quaternions) and 2.2 (Rotations) of Eric Lengyel, Foundations of Game Engine Development, Volume 1: Mathematics. The gizmos and the Euler comparison are my own — not book figures or source.
